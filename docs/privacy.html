Privacy Policy

Offer Calculator (com.offerpk.calculator) · Effective 2026-09-28 · Version 1.0.0-complete

Summary

Offer Calculator is an offline-first calculator. It does not collect personal data, does not require an account, and does not send your calculations to any server.

Data stored on your device

Preferences and calculator state are saved only in your device’s local storage (web localStorage / app sandbox), including:

  • Calculation history (capped at 50 entries)
  • Memory register (MC / MR / M+ / M− / MS)
  • Theme, accent color, and premium unlock flags
  • Settings: angle mode (deg/rad), sound, haptic, keep-screen-on

You can clear this data by clearing the app/site storage in your browser or OS settings, or by using in-app Clear History and resetting preferences.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email, phone, or account
  • No location, contacts, or photos
  • No analytics SDKs in the free offline core
  • Calculations are not uploaded

Ads (optional stubs)

The app may show banner ads only on secondary screens (History and Themes)—never over the keypad and never blocking calculate. A rewarded ad stub can unlock premium themes and remove the banner (one-time). Interstitials, if enabled later, are rare (e.g. after clearing a long history) and never block calculation. When real ad networks are configured by the publisher, those networks may process device advertising identifiers under their own policies. The offline calculator core works with ads disabled.

Children

The app is a general-purpose utility suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated in the app package.

Contact

Publisher: OfferPk / Mia Smith. For privacy questions about this app, contact via the store listing or project repository associated with package com.offerpk.calculator.
